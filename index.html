🐙 Blue-Ringed Octopus
Run to the Sea

You are a tiny Australian blue-ringed octopus, stranded far inland and drying out in the sun. The ocean is somewhere out there — you just don't know which way.

  • Stay wet: your health drains as you dry out. Wade into puddles, streams, ponds, dripping taps, sprinklers and rain showers to recover.
  • Mind the ground: mud is slow but moist; hot asphalt, sand and rock bake you faster.
  • Hide: predators hunt you. Hold Space to camouflage — your body takes on the ground colour, the blue rings fade, and they wander off. Don't hide right under a dog's nose! When a predator notices you, your rings flash in warning.
  • Explore: no map, no compass. Listen for gulls, watch the sand, and notice which way the wind and streams are going.
